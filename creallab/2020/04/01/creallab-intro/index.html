**CReAL** : Coexistent Reality and Intelligent Agents Lab

01 Apr 2020

CReAL (Coexistent Reality and Intelligent Agents Lab) is a research laboratory exploring the boundary between virtual and physical realities, where humans, intelligent agents, and environments coexist and interact seamlessly.

We integrates AI, motion generation, robotics, and XR technologies to build systems capable of understanding and coexisting with humans in both real and virtual spaces.

“Our goal is to create intelligent systems that understand human motion, emotion, and intention — enabling coexistence rather than replacement.”
